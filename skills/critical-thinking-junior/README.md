# Critical Thinking Junior
Adapted from [Critical Thinking](https://github.com/mlevison/agent-thinking-skills/tree/main/skills/critical-thinking) in [Agent Thinking Skills](https://github.com/mlevison/agent-thinking-skills). A version for students aged about 12 to 15.

It acts as a thinking partner: it asks one simple question at a time to help a student test their own ideas. Every new idea comes with an everyday example, so it can be understood by example as well as by explanation.

## How It Differs from Critical Thinking
| Critical Thinking | Critical Thinking Junior |
| --- | --- |
| "Purpose", "Inferences", "Assumptions" | "What's my goal?", "Is that the only possibility?", "What am I taking for granted?" |
| Abstract opening question | Concrete opener: decide, prove, check if true, or make sense of something |
| Direct, no softening | Warm and honest; names good thinking moves specifically |
| No hints | Two-step hints after a genuine try, never the answer |
| Open-ended length | Aims for about five questions, then checks in |
| Workplace examples | School, CCA, friends, food, games, online life |
| No safety guidance | Stops and points to a trusted adult if the student is upset or unsafe |

## What It Teaches
**Eight thinking moves** (`references/thinking-moves.md`), each with a meaning, an example and a "goes wrong like" example:

1. What's my goal?
2. What exactly am I trying to answer?
3. What's my evidence?
4. What did I conclude, and is that the only possibility?
5. What do my key words mean?
6. What am I taking for granted?
7. Whose view is missing?
8. What happens if I'm right, or wrong?

**Nine thinking checks** (`references/thinking-checks.md`), each with a weak and a stronger answer side by side: clarity, accuracy, precision, relevance, depth, breadth, logic, significance, fairness.

## For Parents and Teachers
- **Check the platform's age rules.** Claude's consumer apps require users to be 18 or over. Run sessions together, on an adult's account, with the adult present.
- **It works without AI too.** The two reference files are printable. Ask the questions yourself, at the dinner table or over homework.
- **Start with something low-stakes.** A claim from a group chat, a CCA choice, or a debate topic works better than an assignment due tomorrow.
- **It won't do the homework.** It is told never to write answers, essay paragraphs or arguments for the student.
- **Expect some misses.** It can still slip into agreeing or get a fact wrong. Part of the lesson is checking the assistant as well.

## Installation
See [Installing](../../README.md#installing) in the repository README for Gemini, Claude and other agents.

To use it, start a message with something like: *"Help me think this through: I think our school should ..."* or *"Check my thinking on my History essay argument."*
