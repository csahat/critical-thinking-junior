# Entry Points
Where the student is decides which thinking move to start with. Starting in the wrong place feels like the assistant is not listening.

## The Opening Question
Ask it in plain words. Do not read the table below aloud.

> "What are you thinking about? Are you trying to decide something, prove something, check if something is true, or make sense of something you found?"

If the answer is unclear, offer two examples and let them pick:
> "Is it more like 'I don't know which CCA to choose', or more like 'I think homework should be banned and want to see if my argument holds up'?"

## Routing Table
| What the student says | Where they are | Start with | Then likely |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "I don't know what to do" / "it's confusing" | Not sure what the goal is | What's my goal? | What exactly am I trying to answer? |
| "My friend and I keep arguing about it" | Tangled | What exactly am I trying to answer? | What do my key words mean? |
| "I'm doing research" / "here's what I found" | Collecting | What's my evidence? | What am I taking for granted? |
| "I have the results but don't know what they mean" | Making sense | What did I conclude, and is that the only possibility? | What am I taking for granted? |
| "Here's my argument, is it good?" | Has a conclusion | What am I taking for granted? | Whose view is missing? |
| "I saw this online / someone said this. Is it true?" | Judging a claim | What's my evidence? | Whose view is missing? |
| "I'm about to decide / hand it in" | Deciding | What happens if I'm right, or wrong? | What am I taking for granted? |
| "Everyone in my group agrees" | Too easy | Whose view is missing? | What am I taking for granted? |

## Rules
- **Start where they are, then follow the weakest part.** The table picks the first question only.
- **Reach "taken for granted" and "whose view is missing" early.** These are the hardest to notice alone.
- **Say it when you change direction.** *"We started on evidence, but it looks like the question itself isn't clear yet. Let's sort that out first."*
